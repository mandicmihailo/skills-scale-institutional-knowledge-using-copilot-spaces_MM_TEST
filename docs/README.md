# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the central index for OctoAcme project management documentation. It provides a quick overview of our project lifecycle and links to each process guide used by teams.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme follows a lightweight, repeatable lifecycle designed for cross-functional delivery:

1. **Initiation** — validate the business need, define goals and success metrics, identify stakeholders, and approve moving into planning.
2. **Planning** — break work into a prioritized backlog, estimate scope, map milestones and dependencies, and define acceptance criteria and quality expectations.
3. **Execution & Tracking** — deliver in small increments through team check-ins, pull request reviews, testing, and progress reporting.
4. **Release & Deployment** — complete release checks, deploy and verify changes, communicate the release, and maintain rollback readiness.
5. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** — capture lessons learned, assign improvement actions with owners and due dates, and track follow-through.

Throughout the lifecycle, **Risk Management & Communication** keeps risks and dependencies visible through a risk register, regular stakeholder updates, and clear escalation paths. **Roles & Personas** clarify responsibilities and collaboration expectations across developers, product managers, and project managers.
